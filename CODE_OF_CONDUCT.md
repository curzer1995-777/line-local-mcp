# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, patient, and specific.
- Respect differing viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Protect other people's privacy. Never post real LINE messages, identifiers, databases, keys, memory dumps, or private paths.
- Accept moderator decisions made to protect the project and its participants.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate privacy violations, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in any project space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other material that violates this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report public, non-sensitive concerns through a GitHub issue. For a confidential report, use the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/curzer1995-777/line-local-mcp/security/advisories/new) and state that it is a Code of Conduct report. Do not include third-party private data unless strictly necessary.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, limit disclosure to those needed for resolution, and avoid retaliation against good-faith reporters.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository activity and to public representation of the project in other spaces.
